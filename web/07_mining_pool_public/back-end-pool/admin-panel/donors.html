Donors

Every address that has donated to the prize pool, carries a live donateN tag, or has a donor name on file. A donor's name is the label of their tagged rig (yourbrand-donate10 → yourbrand), captured only when the tag is set from zero. Censoring is per address and sticky — later renames stay hidden until you un-censor, which also tells the auto-list to leave that address alone. Rank and score are what the public wall ranks by (GRIN in the window × loyalty). All dates are UTC.

Donation settings

These are keys of the incentives section (saving is step-up gated, like the Incentives page). Saving a changed word list re-scans every stored name and reports the counts here. Restore defaults on Settings → Incentives resets these too.

Miner donations

Miners donate N % of their payout by naming a worker donate10 (10 %) or yourbrand-donate10. N is clamped 0–100; donate0 pauses. Off → stored percentages are dormant: nothing is taken, the wall shows every donor as paused, and no name is shown.

Donor names

One entry per line, matched inside the name after removing -/_ and the leet digits 0 1 3 4 5 7 (so a55-h0le matches asshole). Substring matching means false positives (classic contains ass) — un-censor those above and they stay allowed. Ten fixed impersonation words (admin, official, operator, support, staff, pool, grinium, prize, jackpot, winner) plus your pool name are always applied whatever this list says. Saving re-scans every stored name.
