Ads

Promotions shown on the public site — a self-hosted banner image, or a native text card (headline + button, no design needed) — each bound to a placement. When a placement has several active ads they rotate automatically, one at a time (interval configurable below).

All ads

Multiple active ads in the same placement rotate on the public site — one at a time, advancing every 8 s. Weight sets the cycle order (highest first); a single ad just shows permanently. Views/clicks are unverified client-reported counters — the public beacon that increments them is unauthenticated by design (no visitor data is stored), so anyone can inflate or deflate them. Treat them as a rough delivery signal, never as a billable measurement.

s

Sidebar layout. In rail mode the sidebar placement is a slim vertical strip fixed in the empty page margin (screens ≥ 1366 px only) — it costs the page layout nothing, peeks out for a while, tucks itself away against the edge, then slides back with the next creative. Visitors can pull it out by its tab or dismiss it for the visit. Use tall creatives here (160×600). The sidebar placement is not shown at all below 1366 px — with no gutter to park it in, a tall banner would drop into the page flow and become a screenful of scrolling on a phone. So keep your mobile inventory in the header, in-content and footer placements.

Starter banners

Self-promo SVG banners shipped with the pool (served from /promo/).
