G-01 Sign in with your mining address

  • IP: sign in from the network your rigs mine from — any IP they sent accepted shares from recently counts.
  • Password: the password in your rig's pool config. Common ones (x, 123, d=…) and anything under 8 characters are never recorded as proof, so with one of those you can sign in by IP only.
  • The pool checks the proof and tells the games yes or no. The games never store it.
  • Good to know: anyone who can give one of these proofs signs in as the address — someone who mined a few shares to it, or who shares your public IP. They could spend your plays or play games in your name. Plays and points are worth nothing outside the games, so this is accepted; chat asks for an older proof.

                  G-05 How plays are earned

                  Plays, points and fair play
                  • Plays come from mining minutes, not hashrate. Every minute your address has an active miner at this pool counts. By default 10 active minutes earn 1 play, up to 24 plays a UTC day and 100 held at once — the same for a small miner and a large one. New plays appear within about 5 minutes.
                  • A game costs a play. By default one game against the bot costs 1 play. A game against another miner costs each side a play; it is refunded if nobody accepts within 3 days, or if the game is aborted before both sides have moved.
                  • Games against people are rated. By default the first 3 games a UTC day between the same two addresses are rated: they move the Elo rating and pay 10 points for a win, 5 for a draw. Further games that day still count as games, not as points, ratings or leaderboard wins. The engine cannot tell whether someone had help; points are for fun.
                  • Points are for fun. A win against the bot pays 1, 2 or 3 points by level; a draw pays half, rounded down. Points are capped per day, and they are never GRIN: they cannot be paid out, sold or moved to another address.
                  • The server keeps the board. Every move is checked by the pool's games service; the board in your browser only draws it. You have a few days per move — run out and the game is lost.
                  • Events are competitions between addresses — most mining minutes, most active days, most wins against the bot. They run on whole UTC days, and the rewards are points and badges, never GRIN.
                  • Chat is for the pool's active miners. By default you need an hour of mining in the last 7 days, and a sign-in proof at least a day old, so someone who only just mined to your address cannot post as you. Links are held for review and never clickable.
                  • Your address is shown to others masked, the way the pool's own pages show it (grin1abcd…wxyz). You can ask for a nickname: the pool reviews it first, and it always shows together with the mask (Night Shift (grin1abcd…wxyz)), never on its own.
