Moderators

A moderator is a miner address you trust to help with the chat. They moderate from /play/ itself, signed in with their own address, and see a moderator queue there. They can delete or approve messages and mute an author for up to 24 hours. They cannot touch your messages or another moderator's, ban, change settings, points or events, or see full addresses. Everything they do is in the moderation log below, under their address. By default they must sign in with their rig password to act (Overview & settings → Moderators). Their messages carry a "Mod" badge.
