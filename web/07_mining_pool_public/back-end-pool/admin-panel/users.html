Counts come from the admin audit log. Attempts refused earlier in the chain — by the rate limiter, an existing IP ban, or the CAPTCHA gate — are rejected before the login route records anything, so they are not counted here. A low number during a suspected flood means the outer defences are absorbing it, not that nothing is happening.

Currently locked or banned

Login lockouts apply to a (username, source address) pair — one attacker grinding your username cannot lock you out from your own address. Both lists live in the running service's memory, so a restart clears them; an empty list right after a deploy does not mean the attempts stopped.
