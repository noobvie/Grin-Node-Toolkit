Review queue

Donor names and banners are set by donors on their account page (both ownership proofs, and only after a first donation). Nothing here is public until you approve it. Flags are hints, never decisions: a reserved word or the pool's name, a word from your flag list, or a name equal to another donor's approved one (impersonation). Approving a name or banner replaces the donor's current one. All dates are UTC.

Every address that has donated to the prize pool, has a rig mining with a live donateN tag, or has a donor profile on file. Donation settings

These are keys of the incentives section (saving is step-up gated, like the Incentives page). Restore defaults on Settings → Incentives resets these too.

Miner donations

A rig named rig01-donate10 (or just donate10) donates 10 % of what that rig's shares earn; the miner's other rigs are unaffected. N is 0–100. Off → every tag is ignored: nothing is taken, the wall shows every donor as paused, and donor profiles cannot be changed.
Shown on the fortune board so anyone can donate. Leave blank to show none. Reflect what arrives there into the prize pool with a manual top-up on Settings → Incentives.

Donor profiles

One entry per line, matched inside a requested name after removing spaces and - _ . & ' and mapping the leet digits 0 1 3 4 5 7 (so a55-h0le matches asshole). A hit only puts a flag on the queued name — you still decide, so false positives (classic contains ass) cost nothing. Ten fixed impersonation words (admin, official, operator, support, staff, pool, grinium, prize, jackpot, winner) plus your pool name are always flagged.
