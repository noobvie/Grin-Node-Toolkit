P-04 Payout controls one gate · amount · Tor or slatepack

🔐 Ownership gate — required for every payout action
Accepted: one of the last two IPs your miner submitted shares from (check yours at tools.grin.money/tools/my-ip), or the password set in your miner's pool config. Common defaults like x or 123 never count — set a real one on your rig.

Withdraw

Sends the amount to this address's wallet over Tor. Wallet reachability: checking…

Generates a slatepack encrypted to your address — only your wallet can open it. Steps: 1) generate, 2) receive it in your wallet, 3) paste the response to finalize.

Delivers the payout straight into your Goblin wallet over Nostr — no Tor listener, no copy/paste. Because this rail pays a username (not this address's own wallet), you must register the destination once and wait out a short security window before the first payout. This gives you time to notice and undo a hijack: if someone who guessed your gate registers their own Goblin name, re-registering yours resets the clock and evicts theirs.

—

One payout at a time — while one is in flight it appears above with its status. Nothing is ever stuck: a Tor payout that can't reach your wallet retries on a schedule and, after the final retry, the amount returns to your balance automatically; an unclaimed slatepack refunds itself when its expiry window passes. There is no manual cancel — in Grin a payout that looks failed can still land, so cancelling one could pay twice. For the same reason, after a payout is returned to your balance a short cooldown applies before you can request the next one (on any method).
