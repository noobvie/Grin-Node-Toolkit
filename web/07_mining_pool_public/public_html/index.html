P-05 Region patch bay — connect here

What do the region colours mean?
online — miners active idle — ready, no miners yet offline — gateway unreachable

Connect to any green or blue region — blue only means it is up and ready with no miners on it yet, not broken. A red region is temporarily unreachable, so just pick another. Your rewards are identical on every region, so choose the one nearest you for the lowest stale-share rate.

P-05b Miner setup — what to type into your ASIC

Open your miner’s web panel (its IP address in a browser) → Miners → Pool Configuration (iPollo shown; other vendors are similar), then fill in the three fields below.

Pool Setting Miners → Pool Configuration
Select CoinGRIN
Pool 1stratum host not configured
Pool1 worker<your_grin_address>.rig1
Pool1 password<your_own_password>
  1. Pool 1 — the endpoint from the patch bay above (updates as you pick a region). Algorithm: Cuckatoo32 (GRIN). If your firmware rejects a bare host:port, prefix it with stratum+tcp://.
  2. Worker — your Grin address; it is your login, no signup. Optionally add .rig1 to name the machine (lowercase letters/digits/-/_, max 25 chars — longer names are trimmed, never rejected).
  3. Password — choose a strong, private password (4+ chars; never x, 123 or firmware defaults). This is your ownership proof for withdrawals, so treat it like a key. Use the same password on every rig under this address — any of them can then prove ownership, and a real password means you can always withdraw even if your IP changes.

Optionally point Pool 2 / Pool 3 at other green or blue regions as automatic backups, then save — accepted shares should appear within a few minutes.

P-08 Operating procedures

  • PPLNS (Pay Per Last N Shares) — rewards follow your recent contributed work
  • No account, email, or registration — your Grin address is your identity
  • Payouts are requested by you (account page) and delivered over Tor or as an encrypted slatepack — nothing is sent unless you ask
  • An unreachable Tor wallet is retried automatically (6h → 48h back-off); undeliverable or unclaimed payouts are returned to your balance
  • Block rewards mature after 1,440 confirmations (~24h) before they are credited
  • Orphaned blocks are detected automatically and their rewards reversed fairly
