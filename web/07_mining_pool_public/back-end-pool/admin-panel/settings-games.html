Games

The /play/ games platform: master switch and chat switch

Master switch

This is one of two switches. The other is the games service's own launch state: a fresh install starts in preview and goes live only when you click Go live on Games → Overview. The pages follow the lower of the two, so Off here always closes /play/, and On here shows the Play link under Community only once the games are live and the service is answering. If the service stops, the link hides by itself within about a minute. Nothing here touches mining: the games run as a separate service with their own database.

Chat

Only has an effect while games are on or in preview. Players can post only after their ownership proof has aged (24 hours by default), so a stranger who mines a few shares to someone else's address cannot speak as them straight away.
