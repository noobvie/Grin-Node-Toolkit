Banner review queue

Donor banners are set by donors on their account page (both ownership proofs, and only after a first donation). No banner is public until you approve it — a word list can check a name, never an image. Approving a banner replaces the donor's current one. Donor names are not reviewed here any more: they are checked automatically and go live at once (see Donor names below). All dates are UTC.

Donor names

A donor name is checked automatically and goes live at once: 2–32 letters, digits, spaces and - _ . & '; no reserved word (admin, official, operator, support, staff, pool, grinium, prize, jackpot, winner, or your pool's name); nothing on the blocked-word list — the built-in list plus your own words on Settings → Names; not a banned name; not a name another donor already uses, in any spelling (Acme & Co. = ACME-co). A donor can change it once every 7 days. Remove takes a name off the wall (the reason is shown to the donor). Ban name removes it from everyone who holds it and stops every spelling of it from being set again. Block stops an address from setting a name or a banner. A hint means the rule would refuse a live name today — for example because of a word you added after it went live; it stays live until you remove it.

Donors

Every address that has donated to the prize pool, has a rig mining with a live donateN tag, or has a donor profile on file. Remove takes a live name or banner off the wall (the reason you give is shown to the donor). Block stops an address from setting a name or submitting a banner and withdraws a banner it has waiting; a live name or banner stays until you remove it. Rank and score are what the public wall ranks by (GRIN in the window × loyalty).

Miner donations

A rig named rig01-donate10 (or just donate10) donates 10 % of what that rig's shares earn; the miner's other rigs are unaffected. N is 0–100. Off → every tag is ignored: nothing is taken, the wall shows every donor as paused, and donor profiles cannot be changed.
