Abandoned balances

Balances of addresses idle past the dormancy window are swept into the community prize pool (never operator revenue), where they are given away through the pool's published draws. Configure the window in Settings → Payout. Disposition is FINAL and honours the payout freeze. All amounts GRIN (ツ), times UTC.
