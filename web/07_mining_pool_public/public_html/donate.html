D-01 Donate a slice of your payouts for miners — no account, no extra step

Option 1 · for miners

Add donate<N> to a rig's worker name, where N is the percentage (0–100) of what that rig earns that goes to the prize pool. A donation is per rig: your other rigs keep everything they earn. The tag can be the whole worker name or a -/_ suffix:

Worker nameResult
grin1…address.rig01-donate10This rig donates 10% of what it earns. Your other rigs are unaffected.
grin1…address.donate10The same, with the tag as the whole worker name
grin1…address.rig01No donation (normal worker). rig01-donate0 is the same.
donate101, donate-1, donatexxIgnored: treated as a normal worker name, donates nothing

To change or stop: rename the rig and restart the miner. It applies from that rig's next share (shares from the last hour keep the old tag if a block is found soon). Anything out of range or mistyped is treated as an ordinary worker name, so a typo can never donate more than you meant. The slice is taken from each block reward as it matures, which is also when you first appear on the wall below. Your account page shows which of your rigs are donating.

D-01b Your donor name & banner optional, reviewed before it appears

The wall below shows your masked address until you choose a name.

  • 🪪 Where: in the Donor profile panel on your account page. It unlocks after your first donation is taken, which happens when a pool block your tagged rig mined for matures.
  • 🔐 Proof: a recent mining IP and your rig's stratum password, both. Someone who only mines to your address cannot set a name for you.
  • 👀 Reviewed first: the pool checks every name and banner before it appears. Nothing you submit is public until it is approved.
  • 🖼️ Banner: the top donors in the league also show a banner. 800 × 200 px is recommended; PNG, JPG or GIF (animation allowed), up to 300 KB.

A name is 2–32 characters: letters, digits, spaces and - _ . & '. A name and banner stop showing some months after your last donation, and come back when you donate again.

Can't pass the check, for example because you mine from a new IP or your rigs have no password? Ask the pool operator. Contact the operator

    D-04 Where the prize pool goes 100% flows back to miners

    The pool fee pays for the servers. It never touches the prize pool. The two are kept completely separate — running costs come out of the fee, and the prize pool exists only to be given back to miners.

    • 💚 Where it comes from — seeded by the operator, grown by miner donations, and joined by any long-abandoned balances the pool sweeps. Nothing is ever paid out of it to the operator.
    • 🎉 Where it goes — community contests and special occasions, announced ahead of time. There is no fixed schedule: draws are held when there is something worth giving away.
    • 🎲 How winners are picked — every draw is seeded from a Grin block hash, published with the result, so anyone can check the winner was not chosen by hand.
    • 🧾 How you can check — the balance is shown at the top of this page and every GRIN in and out is listed below, by source.
