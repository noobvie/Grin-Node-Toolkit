                  G-05 How tickets are earned

                  Tickets, points and fair play
                  • A game costs a ticket. By default one game against the bot costs 1 ticket. A game against another player costs each side a ticket; it is refunded if nobody accepts within 3 days, or if the game is aborted before both sides have moved.
                  • Miners earn tickets from mining minutes, not hashrate. Every minute your address has an active miner at this pool counts. By default 10 active minutes earn 1 ticket, up to 24 tickets a UTC day and 999 held at once — the same for a small miner and a large one. New tickets appear within about 5 minutes.
                  • Everyone else can make a free guest account — a name and a password, no email, no mining. By default a guest has 5 tickets every UTC day: at 00:00 UTC the count goes back to 5, so unused tickets are not saved up. There is no way to recover a forgotten password. A guest account nobody signs in to for 180 days is deleted.
                  • Games against people are rated. By default the first 3 games a UTC day between the same two players are rated: they move the Elo rating and pay 10 points for a win, 5 for a draw. Further games that day still count as games, not as points, ratings or leaderboard wins. The engine cannot tell whether someone had help; points are for fun.
                  • Points are for fun. A win against the bot pays 1, 2 or 3 points by level; a draw pays half, rounded down. Points are capped per day, and they are never GRIN: they cannot be paid out, sold or moved to another account.
                  • The server keeps the board. Every move is checked by the pool's games service; the board in your browser only draws it. You have a few days per move — run out and the game is lost.
                  • Events are competitions — most mining minutes, most active days, most wins against the bot. They run on whole UTC days, and the rewards are points and badges, never GRIN. The mining ones are for miners only.
                  • Chat. Miners need an hour of mining in the last 7 days, and a sign-in proof at least a day old, so someone who only just mined to your address cannot post as you. Guests can post once their account is a day old, at most one message every 15 seconds and 10 an hour. Links are held for review and never clickable.
                  • Names. A miner is shown by their address, masked the way the pool's own pages show it (grin1abcd…wxyz); a guest by their name with a guest tag (NightShift · guest). A miner can add a nickname (letters and digits), which always shows together with the mask (NightShift (grin1abcd…wxyz)), never on its own; a guest can change theirs. Every name is checked automatically and goes live at once; the pool and its moderators can remove one that breaks the rules.
