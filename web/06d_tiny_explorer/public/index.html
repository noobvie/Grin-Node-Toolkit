Why Grin stands out
  • Sound money backed by time. Gold answers to central banks; Bitcoin caps at 21M. Grin emits exactly 1 ツ per second, forever — a constant, predictable heartbeat.
  • Fair launch, endless emission. Flat rewards mean a miner joining today gets the same deal as a day-one miner — no pre-mine, no founder's reward.
  • Privacy by default. Mimblewimble hides amounts and has no addresses on-chain — transactions are confidential out of the box.
  • Lean & scalable. Pruning keeps a full node tiny (~a few GB) — orders of magnitude smaller than BTC/ZEC/XMR.
  • Community-run. No company, no VC — a global crew of developers and enthusiasts.
What can you check here?
  • Confirm a block you mined landed — and watch its 1,440-block maturity tick up.
  • Gauge network health at a glance: hashrate, difficulty, peers, mempool.
  • Estimate mining income from the live hashrate.
  • Prove a payment settled via its kernel excess (Grin's txid equivalent).
  • Verify emission yourself: circulating supply is simply height × 60 ツ — explore it on the Emission & Supply page.
  • Read a slatepack before you act on it with the Slate Inspector — amount, fee, which step it is on, and mainnet vs testnet, decoded in your browser.
