P-04 Payout controls one gate · amount · Tor or slatepack

🔐 Ownership gate — required for every payout action
Accepted: one of the last two IPs your miner submitted shares from (check yours at tools.grin.money/tools/my-ip), or the password set in your miner's pool config.
Why does my rig password matter?

It can't be used to steal your coins. A Tor payout goes only to this address's own wallet and a slatepack is encrypted to this address, so there is nowhere else for the money to go. What the gate stops is a stranger reading the public leaderboard and triggering payouts you never asked for — each one burns a pool-paid network fee and force-moves coins you meant to leave alone.

A password is your backup when your IP changes. IP proof breaks quietly: an ISP re-lease, a router reboot, switching to mobile data, or moving the rig all give you a new one. Only your last two are kept, so once yours has rotated twice you cannot prove ownership until you mine a fresh share from the new address. A password you chose survives all of that.

Why factory defaults are refused. A default like x, xxxx or 123 isn't a secret — thousands of rigs ship with the same value, so accepting it would hand one skeleton key to every account using it. Same for a single repeated character of any length, and for anything under 8 characters (short enough to guess offline). Those are never recorded, and the address keeps working on IP proof alone.

Setting one. Put any private 8–128 character string in your miner's pool password field, use the same one on every rig, and it is recorded on your next accepted share. Changing it is safe — the last two are both accepted, so a rotation never locks you out.

Generates a slatepack encrypted to your address — only your wallet can open it. Steps: 1) generate, 2) receive it in your wallet, 3) paste the response to finalize.

Delivers the payout straight into your Goblin wallet over Nostr — no Tor listener, no copy/paste. Because this rail pays a username (not this address's own wallet), you must register the destination once and wait out a short security window before the first payout. This gives you time to notice and undo a hijack: if someone who guessed your gate registers their own Goblin name, re-registering yours resets the clock and evicts theirs.

A flat withdrawal fee of — ツ is deducted from the amount you request, on every method. It covers the Grin blockchain transaction fee, which on Grin is paid by the sender and is charged by transaction size rather than by the amount sent — so it costs the same whether you withdraw 25 or 2500 ツ. Fewer, larger withdrawals therefore cost you less in total. It is a flat rate, not a pass-through of the exact chain fee: the real fee paid on every payout is published on the payments page. One payout at a time — while one is in flight it shows above with its status. Nothing gets stuck: a payout that can't be delivered returns to your balance automatically once its retry or expiry window passes. There's no manual cancel (a payout that looks failed can still land), and after one is returned a short cooldown applies before your next request on any method.
