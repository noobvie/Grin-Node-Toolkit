Hardware cost, pool payout minimums, stale shares and downtime are not included. The break-even price covers electricity only — it is the floor below which a rig burns money every hour it runs, not the price at which it pays for itself.
How this is calculated
  • Your share. your G/s ÷ network G/s × 86,400 ツ. Grin's reward is flat and blocks come one a minute, so the whole network is paid exactly 86,400 ツ a day — your cut is your fraction of the hashrate.
  • The network figure comes from the chain, not from difficulty. This page uses the 24-hour average hashrate this explorer already computes from block headers (Δtotal_difficulty × 42 ÷ Δt ÷ 16384, the Cuckatoo32 solution rate). Being an average, it is steadier than a per-block reading.
  • Pool fee comes off the top; solo miners leave it at 0. Week and month are 7 and 30 days of the same daily figure.
  • Power cost is watts ÷ 1000 × 24 × price per kWh, and the break-even price is that cost divided by the GRIN you mine in a day.
  • Difficulty does not hold still. Hashrate joining the network shrinks your share, and no calculator can know that in advance — which is why every figure above is an estimate at the current difficulty.
