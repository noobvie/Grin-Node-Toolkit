Games · Events

Competitions between addresses on /play/: most mining minutes, most active days, most wins against the bot. Rewards are game points and badges, never GRIN. Events run on whole UTC days: an event starts at 00:00 UTC on its first day and ends at 24:00 UTC on its last; results are counted 10 minutes after the end. Events work while the games are off, so you can prepare them before opening (Settings → Games).

New event

This event is running: its kind, rules and first day are locked because players are already competing on them. You can still change the title, the last day and the rewards.

Rewards

By rank, best first. Ties are broken by address, so every rank is one address. Badges come from a fixed list. Rewards are paid once, when the results are counted, and are not capped by the daily points limit.
